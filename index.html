What it is

Local Ledger is a single-user application that consolidates the owner's own bank, credit card, and brokerage accounts into one local view: balances, transactions, holdings, cash flow, and net worth over time.

How it works

  • Account data is retrieved through Plaid, a regulated financial data network. Bank usernames and passwords are never seen or stored by this application.
  • All data is stored on a single computer owned and operated by the application's owner. There is no cloud component and no public-facing service.
  • The application is read-only. It cannot move money, place trades, or change anything at any financial institution.
  • Access to the application is limited to the owner, on the owner's own hardware.

Who it is for

This application is operated by its owner for personal use only. It is not offered to the public, has no customers, and collects no information from anyone other than the owner.
